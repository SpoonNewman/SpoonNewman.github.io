# Terms of Service

These terms govern the Hermes Alert SMS service operated by Rease Kessler (rease.dev).

## The service

Hermes Alert sends automated security and privacy notification text messages to the mobile number you enroll. Messages are informational alerts about monitored settings on your devices; they are not promotional. Message frequency varies — alerts are sent only when a monitored setting changes.

## Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply. You are responsible for any charges your carrier assesses for receiving messages.

## Opting out

Reply **STOP** to any message to opt out of the service. After opting out, you will receive no further messages. Reply **HELP** for assistance, or contact <a href="mailto:reasek@mit.edu">reasek@mit.edu</a>.
